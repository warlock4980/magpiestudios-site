About the game

Ninjutsu follows a lone shinobi, Kage Ren, as he grows from a grounded fighter into a near-mythic force. The current prototype is deliberately narrow: prove the combat, movement, visual identity, and slice atmosphere before expanding scope.

Current prototype focus

  • Light and heavy katana attacks
  • Shuriken throw and smoke bomb vanish
  • Focus meter and Shadow Strike prototype
  • Training dummy combat arena
  • Moonlit shrine environment pass

Development notes

This page is meant to track public-facing progress without overstating the state of the game.

Honest labels

Concept trailer material is labeled as concept. Gameplay is reserved for real Unreal Engine capture.

Original IP

Ninjutsu is a Magpie Studios property. The combat and visual goals are inspired by action-game craft, not copied characters or assets.

Scope discipline

The current target is a focused vertical slice: combat, presentation, environment, and a strong trailer-ready identity.
