MacJanitor Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-07 · Effective: 2026-05-07

Magpie Studios LLC ("we," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information MacJanitor collects, what we do with it, and what we never touch.

What MacJanitor sends to third parties

When you ask MacJanitor to analyze your disk, the following information is sent to Anthropic's Claude API for contextual analysis:

  • File and folder paths (e.g., /Users/you/Library/Caches/com.apple.Safari).
  • File and folder sizes (in bytes).
  • The user-profile string you set in MacJanitor's Settings (used to inform Claude's judgment about what's safe to delete for someone with your workflow).

What MacJanitor NEVER sends

  • The contents of any file on your Mac.
  • Anything from ~/Documents, ~/Desktop, ~/Downloads, Mail, Messages, Photos, or other personal-content folders.
  • System-identifying information beyond what appears in folder paths.
  • Your Anthropic API key. (See below.)

API key handling

Your Anthropic API key is stored exclusively in your Mac's Keychain. It is never transmitted to Magpie Studios servers (we have none), never written to disk in plain text, never logged, and never sent anywhere except directly to api.anthropic.com when you initiate an analysis. You can revoke or change the key at any time via MacJanitor → Settings.

Telemetry, analytics, and tracking — in the MacJanitor app

None. MacJanitor has no server-side component. We do not collect crash reports, usage analytics, performance metrics, or behavioral data of any kind from the application itself. The app's only outbound network traffic goes to Anthropic's API when you ask it to analyze your disk.

Separate from the app, our marketing website at magpiestudios.app uses cookie-based analytics with your consent — see the next section for the full disclosure. The analytics applies only to website visits, never to use of the installed app.

Website analytics (magpiestudios.app)

Our marketing website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate visit patterns — how many people visit, which pages they look at, what countries they come from, and which channels they arrive through (e.g. Instagram, LinkedIn, direct).

Consent before any cookies. When you first visit magpiestudios.app, you'll see a small banner asking whether you accept analytics. Until you click "Accept," no analytics cookies are set and Google receives only aggregate, non-identifying signals (via Google's Consent Mode). If you click "Accept," analytics cookies are set so we can count unique returning visitors and see how the site is used over time. If you click "Decline," the cookies are never set and your visit is not individually attributed.

What we DON'T receive from Google Analytics:

  • Your name, email, address, or any other identifying information.
  • Anything you typed into a form, since we don't have any forms on this site.
  • Anything from inside the MacJanitor app itself — this is purely website-visit data.

Google's processing of the data on their side is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. To change your consent later, clear your browser's site data for magpiestudios.app and reload — the consent banner will reappear.

Anthropic's privacy practices

Because MacJanitor sends data directly to Anthropic's API, Anthropic's own privacy policy governs how that data is handled on their side. We encourage you to review it at anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

Payment processing

Purchases of MacJanitor are processed by an external Merchant of Record ("MoR"). The MoR collects and stores your payment information (credit card, billing address, email) under its own privacy policy, which will be linked on the checkout page at the time of purchase. Magpie Studios LLC receives only your email address (for sending the download link and license key) and the country code from your billing address (for tax reporting purposes). We do not see, store, or have access to your payment card details at any point.
